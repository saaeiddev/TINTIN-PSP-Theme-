# TINTIN PSP Theme — Work in progress

Creator: Amir Saeid Dehghan

This repository is an unfinished fan project and is not an official or licensed Tintin release. No installable theme or release exists yet. Do not treat the wallpaper as a completed PTF theme.

## Work completed

- AI-generated raster wallpaper master and 480 × 272, 24-bit BMP export. The master is not a layered or vector source.
- Identified PTF Studio 1.0 as a candidate standard PTF authoring tool. Its published asset presets differ from the initial brief: first-level body 48 × 48 and focus 64 × 64; second-level body 32 × 32 and focus 48 × 48. Confirm against the chosen tool and target firmware before export.
